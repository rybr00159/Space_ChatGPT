# Fresh stellar-source and companion search

No uncatalogued star or proven first discovery is established. The strongest
current leads are two possible unreported Gaia associations among already
catalogued stars. Read [the research report](../../NEW_STAR_SEARCH_REPORT.md).

- [Astrometric pair dossier](companions/README.md): two cleaner candidates,
  four weaker associations, five recognized pairs and shifted-position controls.
- [Image evidence](imaging/README.md): native saturation rejection, one faint
  unverified peak and preserved sensitivity controls.
- [Spectroscopy](../../reports/new_stars/spectroscopy.md): sparse velocity leads,
  failed novelty checks, wavelength-dependent shifts and TESS tests.
- [Infrared investigation](../../reports/new_stars/infrared.md): existing stars,
  known brown dwarfs and a false optical-motion interpretation.
- [Searchable ranked table](../../results/new_stars/ranked_leads.csv) and
  [honest counting summary](../../results/new_stars/search_summary.json).

Raw and intermediate evidence is retained. Do not promote an unknown
association to an uncatalogued star or treat missing catalogue rows as proof
of publication priority.
