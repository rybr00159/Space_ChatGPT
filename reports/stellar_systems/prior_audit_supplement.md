# Recognition of the background source and giant-image eclipse candidates

Checked 2026-10-14 UTC. The exact public query bytes, URLs, timestamps and SHA256 manifests are retained in `data/stellar_systems/prior_audit/`. [prior_audit_supplement_results.csv](prior_audit_supplement_results.csv) contains the four actual catalogued sources: TIC 273879360, giant TIC 260352037, and its neighbors TIC 737466840 and 260352041.

Exact Gaia DR3 source queries returned no rows in the four actual NSS tables (`nss_two_body_orbit`, `nss_acceleration_astro`, `nss_non_linear_spectro`, `nss_vim_fl`) or the EB/classifier/variability-summary tables. Missing solutions are not evidence of singleness.

The two fields were checked in 120-arcsecond cones against current VSX, live ASAS-SN (including nonperiodic/unclassified results), its II/366 mirror, Rowan 2022 EBs, Petrosky 2021 WISE variables, Shi 2022 EA binaries, Tang 2025 eccentric binaries, Battley 2022 YOUNGSTER candidate binaries, SB9, WDS and Tian 2020 wide binaries. These checks found no entries. Exact-TIC TAP queries independently returned no matches in the three expanded TESS catalogs or the two vetted Kostov 2025 TESS EB tables. Complete-table spatial/identifier checks also found no matches in Kostov Tables 3/4 or the Traven 2020 GALAH SB2 catalog, recorded in [scout_fullcatalog_matches.json](../../results/stellar_systems/scout_fullcatalog_matches.json).

**Kostov Table 2 includes TIC 273879360 as an unvetted/unvalidated target.** The preserved row contains only TIC, position and magnitude. It provides no eclipse period or EB classification; it must not be confused with the 7,936 newly vetted EBs in Table 3 or the 2,065 previously known EBs in Table 4. The catalogued source position is already known regardless of its absence from those EB tables.

ExoFOP target responses have empty TOI/CTOI/companion/files arrays. The source-specific Villanova pages report no EB catalog entry. Background TIC 273879360 has no SIMBAD object in the retained cone. Its ExoFOP aliases and selected arXiv metadata aliases returned no report. The giant is already recognized as a star; its retained references/aliases were checked without claiming a previously unknown giant. Indexed alias searches do not exhaust literature full text or supplementary survey products.

The close neighbor TIC 737466840 has proper motion and parallax compatible with the giant, but WDS/Tian catalog nulls do not establish a new bound association. The eclipsing signal has not been securely assigned to that neighbor; an unresolved closer source remains possible. TIC 260352041 is another known faint position, with different proper motion. No new star count is inferred from these existing catalogued positions.

The supported conclusion is finite absence of prior stellar-system classification for two real eclipse signals. Reliability differs: the [background source](../../candidates/stellar_systems/tic273879360/README.md) has independent spatial attribution and very deep direct eclipses; the [giant-image signal](hidden_sources.md) remains unresolved. Novelty and component masses require further review and observations.

Rebuild the table and validate snapshots with `python scripts/stellar_prior_audit.py`; `--download --supplement` retrieves missing public snapshots. The script rejects malformed/failed catalog responses. Rebuild complete-table matches with `python scripts/stellar_scout_novelty.py --full-crossmatch --report`. Public response bodies are reused after SHA verification; none were normalized or altered to pass a check. No paid API calls were used.
